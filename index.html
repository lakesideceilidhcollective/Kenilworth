Folk Music. Dancing. Community.

Kenilworth Folk Festival returns in June 2027 for four days celebrating folk music, traditional dance and everything that makes a great festival. With live performances, ceilidhs, workshops and music sessions across Kenilworth, there's plenty to look forward to.
